TRIPS · UP TO 5

Which trip are you editing?

Every trip has its own airports, dates, filters, price targets, alerts and price history. All trips are searched in every run and share its request budget.

01 · ROUTEFrom where, to where?

              For example out to Bangkok and back from Phuket. Google searches these as multi-city trips, which can be slower or fail more often than round trips. A destination equal to this airport is searched as a normal round trip. Changing it starts a new price history.

              02 · TRAVEL DATESWhen are you leaving?
              Trip ends by

              Every departure day in the window is searched with every trip length; with a return date, each departure day only with the lengths that get you back in time. Each extra day costs requests; see the summary below.

              03 · FLIGHTSWhich flights count?
              Max. travel time per direction

                      Google searches only matching flights, so the website, price history and alerts all show the same selection. With several airlines on one trip, “only these” keeps flights where a selected airline flies at least one leg.

                      Flight times local time at each airport · from – until

                      00:00–24:00 is any time. Google searches only flights inside the windows; “until 22:00” means leaving or landing before 22:00. Changing them starts a new price history.

                      Travellers are adults; Google searches seats for all of them. Prices and price targets stay per person, in EUR; with several travellers the dashboard and alerts also show the total.

                      04 · PRICE ALERTSWhen should it tell you?

                      Price targets per departure airport

                      Optional, for airports whose prices differ: empty fields use the trip's targets above. Alerts and the website judge each flight by the target of its airport.

                      05 · DISPLAYNames on the website

                      Optional. Leave empty to use the name from the airport list. The search always uses the airport code.
